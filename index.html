About

Data leadership that stays hands-on

I am Bob ter Brugge, an independent data product lead based in Amsterdam. Over twelve years I have worked across analytics, analytics engineering, product, and applied AI in startups, scale-ups, and financial services.

I have twice grown multidisciplinary data teams from a small reporting setup into teams of six or seven analysts, engineers, and data scientists. I remain hands-on with SQL, Python, and R across Snowflake and Databricks, dbt and Airflow, Power BI and Tableau, and production AI.

I work best where the question is still ambiguous and the answer needs to become something people can actually use: a decision, operating rhythm, model, integration, or product.
